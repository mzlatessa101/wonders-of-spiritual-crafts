# Wonders of Spiritual Crafts — Product Blueprint

**Creator:** Latessa Jamison  
**Stage:** MVP (frontend demo — mock data, client state, no real auth/backend)

## Vision

A social + learning platform where spiritual people, witches, beginners, and practitioners of many paths (including Vodou/Voodoo practitioners as welcome guests and members) can learn craft, share community, read a spiritual library, receive newsletters, and gather in VIP circles. The tone is inclusive, empowering, and culturally respectful—elegant mystical-modern aesthetics without cheesiness or gatekeeping.

## Audiences

- Beginners curious about witchcraft and spiritual craft  
- Practicing witches and eclectic seekers  
- Learners of astrology, crystals/stones, mediumship, spirit communication, Kundalini, and hypnosis (educational/spiritual framing only)  
- People drawn to elemental work and “bending” as embodiment practice  
- Practitioners tracking lunar phases (synodic month ≈ 29.5 days) for craft timing  
- Practitioners and respectful learners of diasporic and folk paths, including Vodou/Voodoo  
- Readers seeking gnosis and craft library titles  
- Members seeking newsletters and live, twice-weekly connection (VIP)

## Moon cycles (accuracy)

- **Synodic month** (new → new) ≈ **29.5 days** — what we mean by “moon cycle” in craft context  
- **Eight phases in order:** New → Waxing Crescent → First Quarter → Waxing Gibbous → Full → Waning Gibbous → Last Quarter (Third Quarter) → Waning Crescent  
- **Craft rhythm (practice, not science):** intention at new; build while waxing; culmination at full; release while waning  
- **Dark Moon:** optional folk/craft label for late waning / pre-new — not a ninth astronomical phase  
- No medical claims that the Moon controls people  

## VIP Schedule Concept

- **Wednesday evening** — Talk & spell lab (share intentions, light craft together)

## Principles

- Many paths, one respectful table  
- Consent and free-will ethics in spellcraft teaching  
- Cultural humility toward living traditions  
- Hypnosis/trance and Kundalini as personal-growth / spiritual education only—not diagnosis or treatment; no forced awakening  
- Spirit communication taught without fearmongering; strong grounding and closing  
- Night gatherings (séance, Ouija, ancestors, prosperity) hosted by Supreme Witch with consent & boundaries language  
- Elemental “bending” = embodiment & ritual metaphor, not physics claims  
- Lunar literacy stays astronomically ordered; craft associations clearly labeled as practice  
- Library lists titles/blurbs only—never paste copyrighted book text  

## Out of scope (post-MVP)

Real authentication, payments, live video, email delivery, push notifications, moderation tooling, CMS-backed lessons/library, and mobile native apps.
